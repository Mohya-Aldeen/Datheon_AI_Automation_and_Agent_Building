- Hot: budget of $10,000+ AND a specific need described
- Warm: some budget or mid-size company, but the need is vague or the budget unclear

qualification (Hot, Warm or Cold),
score (0-100),
estimated_company_size (short text),
estimated_budget_usd (number, 0 if unknown),
reasoning (one sentence),
talking_points (array of 3 short strings)
